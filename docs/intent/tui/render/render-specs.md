# Render Specs (TUI-RENDER)

## The Frame

- [x] **TUI-RENDER-001**: The system shall derive every frame as a pure function of the game-state snapshot, the shell's view state, and the terminal size, caching nothing between frames and mirroring no value the core owns.
- [x] **TUI-RENDER-002**: The system shall lay the frame out as a header row, a tab-bar row, a body, a ticker of one or more rows, and a hint-bar row, with the body taking whatever height the chrome leaves so the regions always sum to the terminal's height.
- [x] **TUI-RENDER-003**: When an offline banner is present, the system shall take its row from the body rather than adding a row to the frame.
- [x] **TUI-RENDER-004**: Where the terminal is narrower than 80 columns or shorter than 24 rows, the system shall draw only the too-small card, while the simulation continues to advance.
- [x] **TUI-RENDER-016**: The system shall draw the header rule as an underline across every cell of the header row at every width step the header takes, changing no character's colour; and shall draw a pane divider — one column of the single-width `│`, running the full height of the panes it separates and no other row — at each boundary between side-by-side panes, taken from the list's space on its edge facing the sidebar and, where the inspector is a pane of its own, on its edge facing the inspector, whatever occupies the list's space, with that space fitting its lines to the width the dividers leave and nothing else drawn in a divider's cells; both in place, adding no row to the frame and changing no region's size, and no divider where a full-screen overlay takes the body.

## Horizontal Layout

- [x] **TUI-RENDER-005**: Between 80 and 99 columns the system shall draw a sidebar and a list, the inspector appearing only in place of the list when the player calls for it.
- [x] **TUI-RENDER-006**: At 100 columns and wider the system shall draw the sidebar, the list, and the inspector side by side, the inspector taking the remaining width.
- [x] **TUI-RENDER-007**: At 120 columns and wider the system shall cap the list's width and give the surplus to the inspector, so a row's name and cost never separate further than they can be read together.
- [x] **TUI-RENDER-008**: The system shall apply an asymmetric dead-band at each breakpoint — entering a layout at the boundary and leaving it some columns below — so a resize that overshoots and settles does not flip the layout repeatedly.

## The List Viewport

- [x] **TUI-RENDER-009**: The system shall derive the list's scroll position each frame from the cursor's position, keeping the cursor visible with a margin above and below, rather than storing a scroll offset.
- [x] **TUI-RENDER-010**: The system shall scroll group headers with the list rather than pinning them.
- [x] **TUI-RENDER-023**: The system shall draw a group header as one line spanning the list's whole width, every cell in `dim`: from the list's left edge two `─`, a space, the header's words, a space, and `─` repeated to the list's right edge, with no cursor glyph, marker, count, or cost; where the list cannot hold the leading rule, the words, and a space, the words shall be cut as TUI-RENDER-014 cuts a name.
- [x] **TUI-RENDER-011**: When a list is longer than its viewport, the system shall indicate that more rows exist beyond the visible range without spending a column on a scrollbar.

## The Pointer Record

- [x] **TUI-RENDER-025**: With each frame it draws, the system shall yield a pointer record holding the terminal size the frame was drawn at and: for each tab in the tab bar, the cells of its label as drawn — its ★ badge where worn, its digit, and its name where the bar draws it — naming its section, the columns between labels belonging to no tab; for each row of the focused section's list drawn within the list's viewport, the cells of its line from the list's left edge to the width the dividers leave (TUI-RENDER-016), naming the row by its identity (TUI-NAV-008) and its section; the cells of the list's space, naming the focused section's list; while the Journal is drawn, the cells of its region with the scroll at which its last line stands at the region's foot, or none where every line fits; and, while the sidebar is drawn, the cells of its region with the stores it drew from in their order, the place in that order of the first store it drew, how many stores it drew, and the furthest place its first store can stand, or none where every store fits.
- [x] **TUI-RENDER-026**: The system shall name in a frame's pointer record only what that frame drew: no row scrolled out of the viewport or folded in the done group; no group header, done header, overflow note, armed row's summary line, or blank line below the last row as a row; no row and no list where the narrow-mode swap, the palette, the Journal, or help holds the list's space; no sidebar where a full-screen overlay holds the body; and no target at all on a frame that draws the too-small card or the waking screen; and drawing shall consult no pointer record of an earlier frame, so each frame remains the pure function of TUI-RENDER-001.

## Numbers and Names

- [x] **TUI-RENDER-012**: The system shall render the figures of the sidebar and of list rows in the compact form of TUI-RENDER-017 and the figures of the inspector in the exact form of TUI-RENDER-018, so that every figure the sidebar or a list row abbreviates has its exact form in the inspector, a row's on that row and the sidebar's on its store's row in the stores group; shall render an open count prompt's preview and an armed row's consequence summary in the exact form wherever they are drawn; shall draw a store's net rate, in the sidebar and on its stores row in the inspector alike, behind `+` where it is above zero and `-` where it is below zero, with no sign where its magnitude is zero or below one billionth, as the compact form of its size in the sidebar and the exact form of its size in the inspector; and shall right-align each figure that stands in a column — a sidebar store's stock and rate, and a list row's count and its cost's first figure — in a field as wide as its form can be, so that the column's figures end at one column on every line.
- [x] **TUI-RENDER-017**: The system shall render a figure in the compact form as at most four digits, rounded to the nearest with a tie rounding away from zero, with a magnitude marker — `K`, `M`, `B`, `T`, or `Q`, one for each three decades — once the rounded value reaches a thousand, a rounding that reaches a thousand of one marker carrying into the next, and at most three decimal places below one; a value of at least one billionth that rounds to nothing in three places shall read `<0.001`, a value that reaches a thousand `Q` once rounded or is not finite shall read `>999Q`, and zero of either sign, a value whose magnitude is below one billionth, or any value below zero shall read `0`, so that no compact figure is wider than six columns.
- [x] **TUI-RENDER-018**: The system shall render a figure in the exact form as every digit of its whole part, with no magnitude marker, exponent, or digit grouping — a whole part longer than the float's precision written with the fewest digits that read back as the same float and zeros in the places past them — and its fraction rounded to the nearest, a tie rounding away from zero, at three decimal places or at its fourth significant figure, whichever lies further, trailing zeros dropped; a figure whose magnitude is below one billionth, and zero of either sign, shall read `0`, a figure below zero shall otherwise be written behind `-`, and a figure that is not finite shall be stated in words, never printed: on a stores row a cap the core reports as not finite as the store having none (TUI-NAV-080), and any other as beyond reach.
- [x] **TUI-RENDER-019**: The system shall draw each store in the sidebar as two lines — first its name, spelled as its stores row spells it, at the left edge, at least one blank column, and at the right edge its stock right-aligned in six columns, a `/`, and its cap left-aligned in six columns, the `/` and the cap's field left blank where the core reports the store uncapped; then its fill bar at the left edge, its rate (TUI-RENDER-012) right-aligned in a seven-column field after the bar, and while the store is capped the `full` tag after the rate, a space between each — so that every store's stock, `/`, cap, bar, rate, and tag stand at one column, the bar line is never wider than seventeen columns, and only the name shortens (TUI-RENDER-014) to leave the figures their fields; and the sidebar shall be at least seventeen columns wide and at least as wide as the pair's thirteen columns, one blank column, and the narrowest name column at which every two names in the resource vocabulary that differ whole still differ when shortened to it.
- [x] **TUI-RENDER-020**: The system shall draw a list row's owned count and next cost in fields at the row's right after its name, one blank column before each field, each drawing what the inspector's description of the row states under that heading (TUI-NAV-018, TUI-NAV-078, TUI-NAV-073) and left blank where it states nothing, save on a kitten's row, whose fields' place carries the kitten's state as TUI-RENDER-024 draws it — the count field as `×` directly before a compact figure, the two right-aligned together in seven columns, and left blank for a count of zero; the cost field as the price's components in the order the core names them, each a compact figure, a space, and the resource's name spelled as its stores row spells it, components apart by `, `, with no need, have, mark, or wait, its first figure right-aligned in the field's first six columns, the field as wide as six columns, a space, the widest stores-row name in the resource vocabulary, and three columns more, and a cost drawn as the longest leading run of its components that fits the field followed, where any component is left out, by `, …` after the last component drawn — giving a field width only in a section that can list a row whose description states that heading, judged by the section alone and never by which rows are listed or their current figures, and the name taking the width the fields leave, so that names, counts, and first cost figures each stand at one column on every row whatever is in view and no figure is drawn in part.
- [x] **TUI-RENDER-024**: On a kitten's row in the Clowder list, the system shall draw the state TUI-NAV-101 names in the place of the row's count field, the blank column after it, and its cost field: begun at the count field's left edge and left-aligned, so every kitten's word starts at one column; in the row's own style; blank after it to the cost field's end; and whole, never cut, at every width the list takes, the place being fixed by the resource vocabulary as the cost field's width is and wider than every name in the job vocabulary and either state word; the name's column shall keep the width the fields leave, as on every other row, so the word shortens no name and changes nothing about whether TUI-NAV-095's hint fits.
- [ ] **TUI-RENDER-028**: On the tinkerers' bench row in the Crafts list, the system shall draw the text TUI-NAV-157 names in the place of the row's count field, the blank column after it, and its cost field: begun at the count field's left edge and left-aligned, in the row's own style, and blank after it to the cost field's end; where that place cannot hold the whole text, the ` · N waiting` clause shall be left out whole, and where it still cannot, the rest shall be cut as TUI-RENDER-014 cuts a name.
- [x] **TUI-RENDER-021**: While a count prompt is open on a listed row, the system shall draw on that row, beginning at the left edge of its cost field's place or, where the list gives no cost field, of the place one would take, the prompt's count as `×` directly before the digits typed, exactly as typed with leading zeros kept, or `×` alone while none are typed; then, where the preview of TUI-NAV-067 prices components, two blank columns and those components drawn as the cost field's are with each figure in the exact form, and nothing after the count where the preview is the count alone or nothing; where that text is longer than the place, the row's count field, keeping its figure and its seven columns, and its name shall move left by what the text needs, the name shortening as far as one character and its ellipsis; text that still does not fit shall keep the count whole and draw the longest leading run of components that fits followed, where any is left out, by `, …`, or, where not even the first fits, the count, two blank columns, and `…`; every other row shall keep its columns; and where the inspector holds the list's space and carries the prompt (TUI-NAV-035), the prompt's count and preview shall take the same text.
- [x] **TUI-RENDER-022**: The system shall draw in the sidebar the stores the stores group lists (TUI-NAV-049), each whole — both its lines — or not at all, the stores drawn standing in the stores group's order; where the sidebar's lines cannot hold every listed store, it shall draw consecutive listed stores from the first store the view is scrolled to (TUI-NAV-140), as many whole as the lines between its notes hold, with a first line `▴ N more` where N listed stores stand above those drawn and a last line `▾ N more` where N stand below, each N written in full; and it shall begin the stores drawn at the store scrolled to or, where that store stands further down the list, at the earliest store from which every listed store through the last fits beneath the `▴` note, so the sidebar never leaves room for another whole store below its last.
- [x] **TUI-RENDER-027**: Where the stores a sidebar note counts include any wearing `full`, the system shall end that note with ` · M full`, M the count of them that wear it, drawing the note in `dim` save its `M full`, which shall wear `attention`; and where the sidebar is narrower than the whole note, the system shall leave out the note's ` more`, keeping its triangle, its count, and its full count.
- [x] **TUI-RENDER-013**: The system shall scale a number's displayed precision to its magnitude, showing no trailing decimals that imply precision the value does not carry.
- [x] **TUI-RENDER-014**: When a name does not fit its column, the system shall shorten it with an explicit ellipsis so the player can see that text was cut, the full name remaining available in the inspector.

## The Frame Budget

- [x] **TUI-RENDER-015**: The system shall bound each frame's drawing work by what is visible rather than by what exists, so a frame's cost does not grow with the size of the game's content tables.
