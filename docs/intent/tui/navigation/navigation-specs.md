# Navigation & Input Specs (TUI-NAV)

## Key Model

- [x] **TUI-NAV-001**: The system shall map keys only from the tmux-safe set — plain printable characters, Tab, BackTab, Enter, Esc, Space, Backspace, and the four arrow keys — plus Ctrl-C as emergency quit; no other Ctrl chord, Alt/Meta, or function key shall be bound.
- [x] **TUI-NAV-002**: While a screen is focused with no prompt or overlay open, the system shall move the list cursor on ↑/↓ (and j/k), and perform the focused row's primary action once on Enter.
- [x] **TUI-NAV-003**: When Space (or i) is pressed on a focused row, the system shall toggle the inspector for that row (TUI-NAV-017 places the inspector by width, and TUI-NAV-088 states how the key answers where no swap is drawn).
- [x] **TUI-NAV-004**: The system shall treat uppercase as the modifier tier with X its only member — x opening the count prompt and X acting to the maximum affordable on the rows TUI-NAV-011 and TUI-NAV-012 name — and shall bind no other uppercase letter, so an uppercase screen mnemonic is ignored as any unbound key is and never acts in bulk.
- [x] **TUI-NAV-037**: While a count prompt or the palette is open — the palette being a field under this rule, not a full-screen overlay under TUI-NAV-052 — the system shall suspend every global single-key binding, so a digit types rather than jumping to a section: the palette shall take every printable character, digits and Space included, into its query, and the count prompt shall take the digits 0–9 into its count and ignore every other printable character and Space with no text and no footer note. Enter shall go to the field — reaching the row beneath only as the field's own acceptance directs (the count prompt's ×N), never as that row's primary action, and never committing an armed row beneath — and Esc shall cancel the field with no effect.
- [x] **TUI-NAV-058**: While a count prompt or the palette is open, Backspace shall delete the field's last character, doing nothing on an empty field, and never fire its global decrement.
- [x] **TUI-NAV-059**: While a count prompt or the palette is open, the arrow keys, Tab, and BackTab shall do nothing, so neither the list cursor nor the focused section moves beneath a field.
- [x] **TUI-NAV-060**: The system shall never close an open count prompt or palette on a timer: it stays open, the simulation advancing beneath it, until Esc closes it or an Enter the field acts on does — the count prompt's Enter always (applying the count, or cancelling on an empty or zero one), the palette's only when it has a result to jump to.
- [x] **TUI-NAV-061**: When Enter accepts a count prompt or the palette, the system shall not let a held or auto-repeating continuation of that press perform the primary action of the row focused once the field closes — only a distinct Enter, as TUI-NAV-051 requires of a heavy row's commit, acts on that row.
- [x] **TUI-NAV-066**: While a count prompt or the palette is open, the hint bar shall show that field's own keys — for the palette, typing to find, Enter to jump, and Esc to close — in place of any other context's keys, an armed row's beneath the field included.
- [x] **TUI-NAV-052**: While a full-screen overlay (Journal or help) is open, the system shall give the overlay the cursor, section, and action keys for its own navigation and suspend their global meanings, while keeping the speed keys, q, Ctrl-C, and Esc live — so the player can always retime, leave, or close from anywhere.

## Navigation

- [x] **TUI-NAV-005**: The system shall assign sections to the digits 1–0 by a fixed design-time map that never renumbers; pressing a section's digit jumps to it, and pressing the digit of an unrevealed section is a no-op with a footer note.
- [x] **TUI-NAV-006**: The system shall show a section's tab while the section lists at least one selectable row — a group header never counting — or is the section in focus, re-derived from current state and focus, listing the tabs in digit order, 1 through 9 and then 0, each in its fixed slot; and shall badge a tab with ★ from each time it appears in the tab bar until its section is next visited — a visit being any entry into the section: its digit, a section cycle, the palette's jump, or the opening focus of TUI-NAV-024 — no tab present when the game opens counting as having appeared.
- [x] **TUI-NAV-007**: Tab/BackTab, l/h, and →/← shall cycle only revealed sections; ↓/↑ and j/k shall move within the focused list; [ and ] shall jump between the list's group headers.
- [x] **TUI-NAV-085**: While the section in focus lists no selectable row, the system shall keep it in focus with its tab shown, moving focus nowhere on its own and leaving the report banner, any open overlay or field, and the layout as they stand; with no field or full-screen overlay holding the keyboard, Enter, x, X, +/=, and -/Backspace shall find no row and do nothing but leave one footer note, the same for each, that the section holds nothing to act on just now; its own digit shall answer as for any section with a tab, a visit to the section in focus; once it is out of focus with nothing listed its tab shall leave the bar, so a section cycle passes over it and its digit answers as TUI-NAV-005 has it for a section without a tab, until a row returns; and when a row returns to it while it is still in focus, the cursor shall land as TUI-NAV-008 and TUI-NAV-032 have it.
- [x] **TUI-NAV-092**: The system shall draw the label of the tab for the section in focus — its digit and its name, in the full form and the compact strip alike — in reverse video inverting the terminal's own foreground and background (TUI-THEME-007), and shall reverse no other tab's label and no column between tabs, so that exactly one tab is reversed on every frame that draws the tab bar; the reversal shall follow focus alone, staying on the section beneath while a field or full-screen overlay is open, and shall add no column to the bar.
- [x] **TUI-NAV-008**: The system shall remember each section's focused item by content identity (not list index), so returning to a section restores the same item even after intervening reveals inserted rows.
- [x] **TUI-NAV-097**: The system shall list each section's rows in groups, one group for each kind of thing its rows are about — the section's own fixed actions, the rows about no content item, forming one kind, and each kind of content, the kittens, the resources, the colonies, the sites, and the declare list each another — every row of a kind standing contiguous in its kind's one group, an item's several rows adjacent within it, save a finished row of the Ideas section, which stands in that section's done group instead (TUI-NAV-110); within a group, fixed actions in the order TUI-NAV-098 names them, the declare list's no-adventure row first, and every other kind in the order the core lists it, kittens by roster position.
- [x] **TUI-NAV-098**: The system shall stand each section's groups in a fixed order, the same on every frame: Meadow — things to do (gather, forage, rummage), structures, stores; Clowder — jobs, things to do (host a jamboree, pounce), kittens; Ideas — ideas, refinements, rules, done (TUI-NAV-110); Crafts — its crafts as one group; Sunbeams — things to do (bask, reverie, enlighten), rites; Wonders — things to do (hold a parade, refine starshard, refine echo, condense a jar, make a promise), arches; Friends — colonies, things to do (scout a picnic); Sky — sites, things to do (summon a star whale, feed the whale); Dreams — things to do (deep nap), loom upgrades; Legacy — things to do (the Move, pass it down), traditions, adventures.
- [x] **TUI-NAV-099**: Where a section's design gives it more than one group, the system shall head each of its groups that lists at least one row — judged after every gate the core reports, a Teased row counting — with exactly one group header directly above the group's first row, the first group included — a line the cursor never lands on, so TUI-NAV-032's first row is the first row beneath any header — and shall list no header above a group that lists no row, so a section never lists a header alone, save the Ideas section's done header, which is listed exactly while its group holds a row (TUI-NAV-111) and stands alone while that group is folded (TUI-NAV-113); a section whose design gives it one group (Crafts) shall list no group header.
- [x] **TUI-NAV-100**: The system shall word each group header as a short lowercase plural noun phrase naming its group's kind, carrying no figure — save the Ideas section's done header, worded as TUI-NAV-111 has it: `things to do` for a section's fixed actions in every section that has them, `stores` for resources, and for every other kind its plain plural — `structures`, `jobs`, `kittens`, `ideas`, `refinements`, `rules`, `rites`, `arches`, `colonies`, `sites`, `loom upgrades`, `traditions`, `adventures`.
- [x] **TUI-NAV-038**: When / is pressed on a screen with no prompt or overlay open, the system shall open a find-anything palette over the results TUI-NAV-063 defines, narrow them as characters are typed to those whose name, as TUI-NAV-063 has each result show it, contains the query, ignoring case, and on Enter close the palette, move to the top result's section, and focus that item's first row there, performing no row action; Esc shall close the palette with no effect.
- [x] **TUI-NAV-062**: The palette shall match the query as typed, spaces included, ignoring case in every comparison, and order the matching results by closeness — a name equal to the query first, then names beginning with it, then names containing it elsewhere — with ties in tab-bar order and then in each section's list order; a query that is empty or only spaces shall list every result in tab-bar then list order. The list shall be drawn from its top, so the result Enter takes is the first one shown and always on screen.
- [x] **TUI-NAV-063**: The palette's results shall be exactly the items listed in revealed sections, re-derived from current state as the simulation advances: one result per item with a selectable row, an item carrying several rows in its section counting once and group headers never counting — so a Teased item appears and an item with no row never does. Each result shall show its item's name, beside its section's digit, and two items sharing a name shall never be merged into one result.
- [x] **TUI-NAV-064**: When Enter is pressed in the palette with a query that is empty, only spaces, or matches nothing, the system shall do nothing, leaving the palette open with its query as typed.
- [x] **TUI-NAV-065**: When the palette's Enter moves to its top result, the system shall treat the move as navigation — disarming any armed row, dismissing the report banner, and counting as a visit to the section it lands in, as any other navigation does — and shall leave the layout as it was, so a narrow-mode inspector swap stays in place and shows the landed row.

## The Pointer

- [x] **TUI-NAV-125**: The system shall give meaning only to a press of the left mouse button and to a step of the vertical wheel, each with no modifier held; a button's release, a drag, motion with no button down, the right and middle buttons, a horizontal wheel step, and any press or step with Shift, Ctrl, or Alt/Meta held shall do nothing.
- [x] **TUI-NAV-126**: The system shall resolve each pointer event against the pointer record of the last frame drawn (TUI-RENDER-025), and against no layout worked out again for the event, only where that frame was drawn at the terminal's present size; a pointer event at cells where that record names no target, or arriving after a resize that no frame has yet been drawn for, shall do nothing.
- [x] **TUI-NAV-127**: The system shall issue no core command for any pointer event, in any context; a pointer event shall open, close, accept, or cancel no field or full-screen overlay, arm or commit no row, set or clear no footer note, retire no report banner and read no report, and leave the inspector call and the done group as they stand, changing nothing but what TUI-NAV-128 to TUI-NAV-133 and TUI-NAV-145 name, and leaving no footer note where it does nothing.
- [x] **TUI-NAV-128**: While no field or full-screen overlay is open, when the left button is pressed on the label of a tab whose section is not in focus and still has a tab, the system shall move focus to that section as a visit (TUI-NAV-006), the cursor landing as TUI-NAV-008 and TUI-NAV-032 have it and a narrow-mode inspector swap staying in, showing the row landed on; a press on the tab of the section in focus, or on a tab whose section no longer has one, shall do nothing.
- [x] **TUI-NAV-129**: While no field or full-screen overlay is open, when the left button is pressed on the line of a row the record names in the section in focus, and that row is still drawn, the system shall put the cursor on that row, remembered by its identity as TUI-NAV-008 has it, and do nothing else, whatever the row's kind; a press on the row the cursor already rests on, on a row no longer drawn, or on a row of a section no longer in focus shall do nothing.
- [x] **TUI-NAV-130**: While no field or full-screen overlay is open, each wheel step over the focused section's list shall move the cursor one row, down for a step down and up for a step up, as ↓ and ↑ move it — passing over group headers and folded rows and stopping at the list's ends — the list's view following the cursor (TUI-RENDER-009) and never scrolled apart from it; a step where the cursor has no row to move to, or over a list where the cursor rests on no row, shall do nothing; and a press over the sidebar, and a press or step over the header, the report banner, the ticker, the hint bar, a pane divider, or the inspector, as a pane or as the narrow-mode swap, shall do nothing.
- [x] **TUI-NAV-131**: While a count prompt or the palette is open, every pointer event shall do nothing, so the field holds the cursor and the section where they stand as TUI-NAV-059 holds them against the arrow keys, and no click jumps to a palette result or closes or accepts the field.
- [x] **TUI-NAV-132**: While the Journal is open, each wheel step over it shall scroll it one line, down toward older entries and up toward newer, never above its top nor past the scroll the record holds for it (TUI-RENDER-025), ending no arming and leaving the report banner showing; while the Journal or help is open, every press and every other step — a step over anything but the Journal, and every step under help — shall do nothing.
- [x] **TUI-NAV-133**: Where a pointer event moves the cursor off an armed row or moves focus to another section, the system shall end the arming as navigation ends it (TUI-NAV-076), and a pointer event that moves neither shall leave an arming as it stands; and no held or auto-repeating continuation of a press then down shall act on the row a pointer event moved the cursor to — only a distinct press, judged as TUI-NAV-051 judges a heavy row's commit, shall act there.
- [x] **TUI-NAV-145**: While no field or full-screen overlay is open, each wheel step over the sidebar's region shall scroll the sidebar one store, toward its last store for a step down and toward its first for a step up, from where the last frame drew it, stopping at the top and where the last frame's record puts the last store at the sidebar's foot (TUI-RENDER-025).

## Scrolling the Sidebar

- [x] **TUI-NAV-140**: The system shall hold the sidebar's scroll as view state — the first store the sidebar is scrolled to, by its identity, or the top where it is scrolled all the way up — opening every sitting, a new game and a load alike, at the top and saving nothing of it; within a sitting it shall change only by a sidebar scroll (TUI-NAV-141, TUI-NAV-142, TUI-NAV-145), a change of section, an overlay, and a resize each leaving it as it stands, and shall be taken as the top where the store it names is no longer listed.
- [x] **TUI-NAV-141**: When `}` is pressed while the global table holds the keyboard, the system shall scroll the sidebar toward its last store by the sidebar step (TUI-NAV-143), from the first store the last frame drew in it, stopping where the last frame's record puts the last store at the sidebar's foot (TUI-RENDER-025).
- [x] **TUI-NAV-142**: When `{` is pressed while the global table holds the keyboard, the system shall scroll the sidebar toward its first store by the sidebar step (TUI-NAV-143), from the first store the last frame drew in it, stopping at the top.
- [x] **TUI-NAV-143**: The system shall take as the sidebar step three stores, or one fewer than the stores the last frame drew in the sidebar where that is fewer than three, and never fewer than one store.
- [x] **TUI-NAV-144**: A sidebar scroll, by key or wheel, shall issue no command, end no arming, retire no report banner, count as no visit, set no footer note, and move neither the cursor nor the section; and a sidebar scroll that cannot move, or that finds no sidebar in the last frame drawn, shall change nothing and leave no footer note.
- [x] **TUI-NAV-146**: Wherever the hint bar shows the focused row's keys, the cursor resting on no row included, and the sidebar has stores out of view, the system shall name the sidebar's scroll keys there as `{} stores`, after every other context key, the done key included, and directly before the help key, as the first entry given up when the bar's width runs short; and the help overlay shall list them among the global keys as `{ / }`, described as `Scroll the stores sidebar up / down`.

## The Done Group (Ideas)

- [x] **TUI-NAV-110**: In the Ideas section, the system shall treat a row as finished exactly where the core's lock reason for its item names the item already the player's — the answer behind the row's ✓ (TUI-NAV-053) — judging it by no rule of its own, and shall list every finished row in one done group standing after the section's ideas, refinements, and rules groups and in no other group: finished ideas first, then refinements, then rules, each kind in the order the core lists it; no section but Ideas shall have a done group.
- [x] **TUI-NAV-111**: While the Ideas section's done group holds at least one row, folded or open, the system shall head it with one group header whose words are `done`, then the number of rows the group holds in parentheses, then ` · d to show` while the group is folded or ` · d to fold` while it is open — as in `done (12) · d to show` — re-derived on every frame; where the list cannot hold the leading rule, all of those words, and a space, the header shall leave out the ` · d to show` or ` · d to fold` clause whole and draw the rest as TUI-RENDER-023 draws a header's words; and while the group holds no row, the section shall list neither the done group nor its header.
- [x] **TUI-NAV-112**: The system shall open every sitting, a new game and a load alike, with the done group folded, saving nothing of its state and storing no setting for it; within a sitting it shall change only on d (TUI-NAV-115), the palette's jump (TUI-NAV-121), or its leaving the list — leaving the section and returning, a resize, Esc, and the simulation advancing each leaving it as it stands — and a done group that stops being listed shall be folded when it is next listed.
- [x] **TUI-NAV-113**: While the done group is folded, the system shall draw its header and none of its rows, and the cursor shall visit none of those rows — ↓/↑ and j/k passing over them, [ and ] passing over its header, and every landing, TUI-NAV-032's included, choosing only among drawn rows — so the cursor never rests on a row that is not drawn; while it is open, the system shall draw its rows beneath its header, and the cursor shall visit them, [ and ] jump to them, and their keys answer, as for any rows.
- [x] **TUI-NAV-114**: The system shall count a row folded in the done group as listed for its section's tab (TUI-NAV-006) and for the palette's results (TUI-NAV-063), exactly as an unfolded row is counted.
- [x] **TUI-NAV-115**: While the Ideas section is in focus, its done group is listed, and no field or full-screen overlay holds the keyboard, the system shall bind d to open the done group where it is folded and fold it where it is open, changing it once per distinct press, judged as TUI-NAV-051 judges a distinct Enter, so that a held or auto-repeating d changes it once; a press shall issue no command, end no arming, retire no report banner, count as no visit, and leave the layout, a narrow-mode inspector swap included, as it stood. On any other screen, or where the done group is not listed, d shall be ignored as an unbound key, with no footer note; while a field is open, d shall be taken as TUI-NAV-037 has it, and while a full-screen overlay is open, suspended as TUI-NAV-052 has it.
- [x] **TUI-NAV-116**: When d opens the done group, the system shall leave the cursor on the row it rests on, or, where it rests on no row, land it as TUI-NAV-032 has it; when d folds the done group while the cursor rests on one of its rows, the system shall move the cursor to the last row drawn above the done group's header, or rest it on no row where no row is drawn above that header.
- [x] **TUI-NAV-117**: While the done group is folded, where the row the cursor rests on in the Ideas section becomes finished — or where, on entering the section, the row remembered for it (TUI-NAV-008) is finished — the system shall place the cursor on the first drawn row that follows that row's place in the section's list, its place being the one it held in its own kind's group in the order the core lists that kind; where no drawn row follows that place, on the last drawn row before it; and where neither exists, on no row; the move shall not be navigation, retiring no report banner and counting as no visit.
- [x] **TUI-NAV-118**: While the done group is open, when the row the cursor rests on becomes finished, the system shall keep the cursor on that row, now drawn in the done group, as TUI-NAV-010 keeps the cursor on its row after an act.
- [x] **TUI-NAV-119**: When TUI-NAV-117 moves the cursor off a row that became finished, the system shall let no held or auto-repeating continuation of a press then down act on the row the cursor moves to; only a distinct press, judged as TUI-NAV-051 judges a heavy row's commit, shall act there.
- [x] **TUI-NAV-120**: While every row the Ideas section lists stands in its folded done group, the system shall draw the done header alone in the list, rest the cursor on no row, keep the section's tab, and answer Enter, x, X, +/=, and -/Backspace as TUI-NAV-085 has them for a section that lists no selectable row; when a row the cursor can visit is next drawn there, the cursor shall land on the first such row that is Available and not finished, else the first such row, as TUI-NAV-032 lands it.
- [x] **TUI-NAV-121**: When the palette's Enter jumps to an item whose row is folded in the done group, the system shall open the done group and focus that row, the jump otherwise answering as TUI-NAV-038 and TUI-NAV-065 have it.
- [x] **TUI-NAV-122**: Wherever the hint bar shows the focused row's keys and d is live (TUI-NAV-115), the cursor resting on no row included, the system shall name d as `d show done` while the done group is folded and `d fold done` while it is open, after every other context key save the sidebar's scroll keys (TUI-NAV-146) and before them and the help key; and the help overlay shall list it among the Ideas screen's keys as `d show or fold done`.
- [x] **TUI-NAV-123**: The system shall make d no screen's mnemonic and no row-bound key, so that d means nothing on any screen but the Ideas section's done key.

## Action Model

- [x] **TUI-NAV-009**: The system shall render each actionable item as a row carrying a glyph-first availability marker (● affordable, ◐ partial, ○ revealed-unaffordable, ✓ owned/done), the flavor name — save on a row where TUI-NAV-089 draws a label in the name's place, and followed within the name's column on a kitten's row by the hint TUI-NAV-095 draws there — owned count, and next cost, save on a kitten's row, which states neither and whose place for them carries the state TUI-NAV-101 names; Teased rows render dimmed with their marker and Hidden content is not rendered.
- [x] **TUI-NAV-089**: Where an item carries several rows in its section — a craft's make and tinkerer count rows, a colony's trade and pen-pal rows — the system shall keep each as a row of its own, draw the item's name on the first of those rows that is listed, and draw on each later listed row, in the name's place, a plain-word label with no glyph saying what that row does — `tinkerers` on a craft's tinkerer count row, `send a pen-pal` on a colony's pen-pal row — the label being its row's own and never its item's name, so that no two of an item's rows read alike and no list shows one item's name twice.
- [x] **TUI-NAV-093**: The system shall list each kitten in the roster as exactly one row in the Clowder section, drawn with the kitten's name and no label, whose Enter promotes that kitten and for which the Clowder screen's p stands as a focused-item mnemonic live on that row (TUI-NAV-046), and shall give no kitten a second row — so no list shows one kitten twice, and AppointElder is taken only by the row-bound key of TUI-NAV-094.
- [x] **TUI-NAV-094**: While the cursor is on a kitten's row, the core reports the command gate of AppointElder for that row's kitten open (SIM-ENG-045), and no field or full-screen overlay holds the keyboard, the system shall bind n as that row's own action key: a press shall issue AppointElder for the kitten the row is about once, the core arbitrating and a refusal surfacing as TUI-NAV-014's footer, moving neither the cursor nor the focused section and leaving the report banner and the layout, a narrow-mode inspector swap included, as they stood, and while n is held or auto-repeats it shall act no more than ten times per second regardless of the terminal's key-repeat rate. On any other row or screen, where the section in focus lists no row, or while that gate is closed, n shall be ignored as an unbound key, with no footer note.
- [x] **TUI-NAV-095**: While the core reports the command gate of AppointElder for a listed kitten row's kitten open (SIM-ENG-045) and that kitten is not the one the core holds as the Elder, the system shall draw on that row, the cursor's and every other alike, the hint `(n: Elder)` after the kitten's name within the name's column, in the dim role and of single-width characters only, and never in the count or cost field; the hint shall be drawn whole or not at all and shall never cost the name a character, so where the name's column cannot hold the whole name and the whole hint the row shall carry no part of the hint and draw its name as TUI-RENDER-014 has it; and a row whose kitten's gate the core reports closed, and the row of the kitten the core holds as the Elder (TUI-NAV-104), shall carry no hint.
- [x] **TUI-NAV-101**: The system shall name on every listed kitten row exactly one state, read from the kitten's location and job as the core holds them with no rule of the shell's own: `visiting` where its location is anything but home, whether or not it holds a job; otherwise, where it holds a job, that job's name spelled as the job's definition names it and its count row draws it, a tinkerer's included; otherwise `idle` — re-derived on every frame and drawn as TUI-RENDER-024 places it; the word shall be no part of the kitten's name, so the palette's result and the inspector's name for the kitten are unchanged, and shall say nothing of the kitten being the Elder, leaving the `(n: Elder)` hint of TUI-NAV-095 as it stands.
- [x] **TUI-NAV-096**: Wherever the hint bar shows the focused row's keys, n is live on the focused kitten's row (TUI-NAV-094), and that row's kitten is not the one the core holds as the Elder, the system shall name it there as `n appoint Elder`, directly after the key that names Enter and ranked for width as a focused-item mnemonic is; while the cursor is on the row of the kitten the core holds as the Elder, the hint bar shall not name n, the key staying live there as TUI-NAV-094 has it; and the help overlay shall list it among the Clowder screen's keys as `n appoint Elder (kitten)`.
- [x] **TUI-NAV-104**: While the core holds a kitten as the Elder (SIM-KIT-022) and that kitten's row is listed, the system shall draw the Elder's mark, in the form of TUI-NAV-105 and as far as TUI-NAV-106 finds it room, on that kitten's row and on no other row — the row for the roster position the core holds, read as it stands with no rule of the shell's own and re-derived on every frame, so that on the frame an appointment lands no row but the new Elder's carries the mark — whatever that kitten's location or job and whatever the command gate of AppointElder reports; and while the core holds no Elder, no row shall carry the mark.
- [x] **TUI-NAV-105**: The system shall draw the Elder's mark as `◆ Elder` — the glyph, one blank column, and the word, of single-width characters only — in the row's own style as the name is drawn and with no palette colour of its own, after the kitten's name within the name's column, placed after the name as TUI-NAV-095's hint is placed on every other kitten's row — after one blank column, while no render-leaf spec places the two otherwise — and in no part of the row's count or cost fields' place, the state of TUI-NAV-101 being drawn there as on every kitten's row (TUI-RENDER-024); the mark shall be its row's own and no part of the kitten's name, so the palette's result for the kitten (TUI-NAV-063) shows the name alone.

- [x] **TUI-NAV-087**: Where the terminal is narrower than 80 columns or shorter than 24 rows, the too-small card shall draw its one line — a calm statement that nothing is wrong and of the size the game needs, 80 columns by 24 rows — whole, wherever the pane is at least as many columns wide as that line and at least one row tall.

- [x] **TUI-NAV-048**: Where the terminal is about 120 columns or wider, the system shall lay out three columns — sidebar, list capped for scannability, and inspector taking the remainder — giving extra width to the inspector and the ticker.
