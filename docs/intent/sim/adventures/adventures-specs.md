# Adventures Specs (SIM-ADV)

- [x] **SIM-ADV-001**: When DeclareAdventure(id) is applied after the Adventures surface is unlocked (the Adventurous Spirit prestige tradition, or the same-named curiosity idea — either opens the surface), the system shall arm that Adventure for the next Move, replacing any previously armed one; DeclareAdventure(None) disarms; before the surface is unlocked it rejects with Locked.
- [x] **SIM-ADV-002**: On a Move, the system shall make the armed Adventure active for the new meadow (recording it in a persisted active_adventure slot) — and, where none is armed, leave no Adventure active, whatever Adventure the old meadow had active and whether or not it was completed — and clear the armed slot; the active twist is exposed via engine-owned twist queries that each affected leaf reads from one place (Long Winter forces season() to Winter; Nap Time halves job production; Silent Sun makes ceremony commands Locked; Gentle Paws zeroes pounce yields; Quiet Retreat blocks jamborees; Frugal Meadow raises energy consumption +10%; Tidy Meadow raises mess accrual) — no leaf hacks its own copy of the twist.
- [x] **SIM-ADV-003**: The system shall define exactly the adventures of the adventures design's catalogue — twist, goal, and reward — with each twist being a slowdown or removal of options only, never a deletion of kittens, structures, or resources.
- [x] **SIM-ADV-004**: While an Adventure is active and its goal is met — judged at each day's close, save a goal that asks for a Move (Silent Sun's, a Memory Chest structure built in the meadow, and Gentle Paws's, the Pen Pal Networks idea researched), judged only at the Move against the meadow being left, before its reset — the system shall complete it once per meadow — incrementing its completion count and growing its reward pool by the reward amount under a per-reward LimitedDR cap — and lift the twist at the following Move; a never-completed Adventure carries no penalty.
- [x] **SIM-ADV-005**: The system shall apply Adventure rewards — every one but Tidy Meadow's one-shot unlock (SIM-ADV-007) — through the typed effect-key system, permanently and cumulatively (LimitedDR-capped per reward), surviving every subsequent Move.
- [x] **SIM-ADV-006**: The system shall give every Adventure a blurb — a short joke, then in plain words its twist, its goal, and its reward as two upper bounds: the reward's per-completion amount as the most one completion adds, and the reward's cap as the most every completion together comes to — stating only what the core applies, each figure equal to the figure the core applies, naming each structure or idea its goal asks for by that item's definition name, and marking each resource it names as SIM-CUR-014 marks them.
- [x] **SIM-ADV-007**: The system shall make Tidy Meadow's reward the one-shot unlock of the Tidy Paws tradition: until Tidy Meadow has been completed at least once, Tidy Paws shall be unrevealed and BuyTradition(TidyPaws) shall reject with Locked, whatever Elder Wisdom is held; from its first completion on, Tidy Paws shall be revealed and bought as any tradition is (SIM-MOVE-010), and later completions shall add nothing more.
- [x] **SIM-ADV-008**: The system shall expose a read-only query reporting whether the Adventures surface is open — the Adventurous Spirit tradition held, or the same-named curiosity idea known — and DeclareAdventure's Locked check (SIM-ADV-001) shall be that query's answer.
