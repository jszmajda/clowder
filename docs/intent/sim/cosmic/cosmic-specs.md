# Cosmic Specs (SIM-SKY)

## The Site Ladder

- [ ] **SIM-SKY-001**: When LaunchExpedition(site) is applied for the next unreached site (the previous site reached, or Fence Top first) after the cosmic surface (the Skyward Longing idea) is unlocked, with the site's mission fuel affordable, the system shall spend the fuel and mark the site reached; an out-of-order or unaffordable launch rejects with Locked or InsufficientResources respectively.
- [ ] **SIM-SKY-002**: The system shall define exactly the thirteen sites of the cosmic design's ladder — mission fuels and the one outpost structure each hosts (economy ids 40–52) — in order, each reachable only after its predecessor.
- [ ] **SIM-SKY-003**: The system shall gate each site's structures on that site being reached (Locked until then), and never un-reach a site.

## Outdoor Production

- [ ] **SIM-SKY-004**: The system shall route site-structure material output through a distinct outdoor channel that takes the global, Elder Wisdom, and Long Sun layers at a reduced transfer fraction (not the full meadow multiplier), and site structures shall consume energy per their definitions.

## Skymaps & Hushfelt

- [ ] **SIM-SKY-005**: The system shall produce skymaps from Lookout Posts, Weather Vanes, and Sky Docks (in addition to the wave-2 Stargazing Ledge and sky events) and grant skymap storage (Lookout Post +500, Weather Vane +2000, Sky Dock +20000) so the mission ladder's skymap costs are holdable; and produce hushfelt from the Hushfelt Grotto and Whisper Post, each granting hushfelt storage so the Memory Chest (700 hushfelt) and Dreamweave become buildable.
- [ ] **SIM-SKY-008**: The system shall grant Jars-of-Purrs storage from the outer outposts (Aurora Lantern +50, Star Nursery +100, Far Beacon +500 each) so the Jar-priced sites and the Distant Dawn loom are reachable — the cap raiser the mystic leaf deferred to this wave.

## The Star Whale

- [ ] **SIM-SKY-006**: When SummonStarWhale is applied after the Sky Dock is built and the Skyward Song idea is researched, with its curiosity + echo cost affordable, the system shall spend the cost and begin a Star Whale visit lasting 5 years plus 1 per contentment point; before those prerequisites it rejects with Locked.
- [ ] **SIM-SKY-007**: While a Star Whale is visiting, FeedWhale shall spend a small dreamcorn lullaby to raise the whale's contentment (lengthening the visit and improving its trades); a whale fed nothing simply departs when its visit ends, never angry, and Trade with the Star Whale colony shall exchange hushfelt for cosmic goods (skymaps, starshards, echoes) only while it visits.
